What Paradox is  ·  Innovation

Paradox tests TBO's platform after every deploy and catches the bugs that quietly lose money.

2  Our idea · the innovation

Check the outcome, not the input.

Paradox knows 12 rules that every correct booking platform must keep. For example:

An agency never ends up richer from its own actions.
The same booking never costs less.
One agency never sees another agency's data.

It replays real user journeys with one sneaky change, then checks whether any rule broke.

3  Why it works

A broken rule is proof, not a guess.

The outcome can't lie. If the wallet grew, money was created, whatever the server replied.
Checked three times on fresh bookings before anything is reported.
Leaks prove themselves. Every booking it makes carries planted fake names and passports.
Priced in rupees, so the team fixes the costliest bug first.
Result: 0 false alarms
